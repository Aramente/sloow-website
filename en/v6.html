Questions

You might be wondering

How long before I see results?

The immediate effect (heart rate slowing) is felt from the first session. Lasting benefits appear after 1-2 weeks of regular practice.

Do I need to practice every day?

Ideally, one 5-minute session per day. But even 3 times a week, you'll see results. Consistency matters more than duration.

Is it compatible with my treatment?

Heart coherence is a relaxation technique with no known contraindications. It can complement medical care but doesn't replace it.

What if I miss a day?

No pressure. The benefits you've gained don't disappear overnight. Just pick up again when you can.
