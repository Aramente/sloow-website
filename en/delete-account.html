Account deletion and associated data removal

Deletion procedure

You can request the complete deletion of your Sloow account and all associated data using one of the methods below:

Method 1: From the app

1 Open Sloow and go to the Profile tab

2 Tap My account

3 Scroll down and tap Delete my account

4 Confirm the deletion. Your account and all your data will be deleted within 30 days.

Method 2: By email

Send an email to kevin.duchier@sloow.app with:

  • Subject: "Account deletion"
  • The email address associated with your Sloow account

We will process your request within 30 days and send you a confirmation email.

Send a deletion request

Data deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently erased:

Data type Action Timeline
Email address and credentials Deleted 30 days
HRV data (RMSSD, SDNN, RR intervals) Deleted 30 days
Session history Deleted 30 days
Program progress Deleted 30 days
Questionnaire responses Deleted 30 days
Preferences and settings Deleted 30 days
Anonymized aggregate data Retained —

Data retained after deletion

Only anonymized and aggregated data (general usage statistics, not linked to your identity) may be retained to improve the service. This data cannot be used to identify you in any way.

Important information

  • Deletion is irreversible. Once the 30-day period has passed, your data cannot be recovered.
  • If you have an active subscription, please cancel it via the Google Play Store or App Store before deleting your account.
  • You can create a new account at any time after deletion, but your previous data will not be restored.
