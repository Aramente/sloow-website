Questions

Let's clarify

Meditation doesn't work for me. Why would Sloow be different?

Meditation requires conscious effort to "calm your mind"  which is exactly what's hard when you're anxious. Sloow bypasses the problem by sending a signal directly to your nervous system. No concentration required.

Is it really effective in 5 minutes?

The physiological effect (heart rate slowing, parasympathetic activation) is measurable from the first session. Lasting benefits come with regular practice.

What do I need to start?

A smartphone and headphones. For advanced biofeedback, you can add a heart rate sensor (like Polar H10), but it's not required to get started.

Is it a medical device?

No. Sloow is a wellness tool based on relaxation and stress management. It's not designed to diagnose or treat medical conditions.
