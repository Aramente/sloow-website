Questions

You might be wondering

What's the difference from meditation?

Meditation requires conscious effort — "calm your mind." Sloow sends an audio signal that automatically regulates your nervous system. You don't have to do anything.

Does it really work in 5 minutes?

The immediate physiological effect (heart rate slowing, muscle relaxation) is felt from the first session. Lasting effects come with regular practice.

Is it a medical device?

No. Sloow is a wellness tool based on relaxation and stress management. It's not designed to diagnose or treat medical conditions.

Do I need special equipment?

A smartphone and headphones are enough. For advanced biofeedback, you can add a heart rate sensor (like Polar H10), but it's not required.
