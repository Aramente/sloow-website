The science

Neural entrainment: your brain follows the rhythm

In 1657, Huygens observed that two clocks on the same shelf eventually beat together. This phenomenon is called entrainment — and your brain works the same way.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Sloow measure my stress?

Sloow uses your phone's camera to detect micro-variations in your skin color caused by your heartbeat (a technique called PPG). From this data, we calculate your heart rate variability — an indicator of your relaxation level.

Why 6 breaths per minute?

It's the resonance frequency of the cardio-respiratory system. At this rhythm (~0.1 Hz), your heart and breath synchronize naturally, maximizing your heart rate variability. It's the principle of cardiac coherence, validated by hundreds of studies.

What's different from other apps?

Most apps ask you to follow an animation or listen to a guided voice. It's tiring and requires concentration. Sloow uses sound that naturally pulses at 6 cycles/minute — your body aligns effortlessly, like metronomes synchronizing.

How long is a session?

A typical session lasts 10 to 15 minutes. You can see the effects on your heart rate variability in real-time, and the app suggests when you've reached a calm state.
