What we measure

3 indicators, 20 seconds

💓

Heart Rate

How many times your heart beats per minute. A basic indicator of your current state.

📊

Variability (HRV)

The gap between each beat. The more variable, the more adaptable your nervous system is.

🌊

Coherence

The synchronization between your heart and breathing. When in sync, your body recovers.

How it works

The science behind

1

Your camera detects micro color variations in your skin with each heartbeat.

2

Our algorithm analyzes these variations to extract your heart rate and its variability.

3

We calculate your coherence — how synchronized your heart and breathing are.

This technique is called photoplethysmography (rPPG). It has been used in research for over 10 years.
